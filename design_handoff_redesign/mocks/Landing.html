What it does

A planner that thinks with you,
not at you.

Four surfaces, one intention: get you from "we should go somewhere" to a bookable plan you trust — without the tab graveyard.

01 / Setup

Tell it cities and dates. It finds the rest.

Context-aware suggestions respect your pace, party size, and budget. No generic top-10s. Every recommendation cites why it fits your profile.

✓ Slow mornings ✓ Food-forward ✓ Mid-budget ✓ Walking over transit + Prefer rooftop views ✓ No early flights ✓ Vegetarian options
02 / Review

Swipe through suggestions
like a senior-editor deck.

Every activity is a card: why it fits, what it costs, how long. One tap to approve, skip, or ask for more like this.

24 suggested ✓ 18 approved
03 / Arrange

Days arrange themselves, geographically.

Drag or let auto-arrange group nearby activities into realistic days. Commute times are real, not vibes.

04 / Finalize

Trip Health runs before you book.

Overlapping dates, thin mornings, missing confirmations — flagged and fixable, ahead of time. Sync to calendar when you're done.

The workflow

Four steps, one unhurried afternoon.

Try it →
STEP 01→

Set the stage.

Cities, dates, party size, budget. Two minutes. GuideMe loads your profile automatically.

STEP 02→

Curate suggestions.

Review activities city by city. Approve what you love, skip the rest. No decision fatigue.

STEP 03→

Compose the days.

Drag activities into days or let auto-arrange do it. Commute times and opening hours are real.

STEP 04·

Ship it.

Trip Health checks for conflicts. Export to calendar. Share with your party. Start packing.
