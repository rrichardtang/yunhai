A few honest questions.

What happens to my trips if I downgrade?
Nothing disappears. Existing trips stay viewable and exportable on Free. You just can't have more than one active or use Pro-only features (auto-arrange, Trip Health, collaboration) on new ones until you upgrade again.
Is there a free trial of Pro?
Yes. 14 days, no card required. You'll get a reminder 3 days before it ends. If you don't upgrade, your account simply reverts to Free.
Do you sell my travel data?
Never. Your profile, trips, and notes are used to make your plans better and nothing else. We don't sell, share, or train external models on your data.
What if I'm planning one big trip a year? Do I really need Pro?
Probably not. Free is genuinely useful for a single short trip. Pro pays off when you're juggling multiple trips, traveling with others, or want Trip Health to catch things before you book.
